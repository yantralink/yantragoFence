Privacy Policy

YantraGO · Effective date: 1 February 2026 · Last updated: 1 February 2026

YantraGO ("YantraGO", "we", "us") operates a GPS-based machine and fleet tracking service consisting of the YantraGO mobile application, the YantraGO admin web console, and the YantraGO cloud backend. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how you can exercise your rights — including your right to have your data deleted.

How accounts work. YantraGO accounts are created by an organisation administrator — there is no self-service sign-up in the app. If your employer or fleet operator gave you YantraGO credentials, that organisation is the account holder; we process your data to provide the tracking service to them.

2. Data we process

  • Account & identity data — name, work email, phone number, organisation, role, and password (stored only as a BCrypt hash — we never store plaintext passwords).
  • Machine & device data — tracker IMEI/device ID, machine name, model and registration details entered by your organisation.
  • Precise machine location — GPS coordinates of tracked machines, reported by the installed tracker (not by your phone) while the tracker is powered, together with telemetry such as speed, ignition state, battery/voltage and GSM signal.
  • Alerts, commands & audit records — geofence and theft alerts, commands sent to trackers, and security/audit logs of actions performed in the system.
  • Push-notification data — Firebase Cloud Messaging device tokens used to deliver alerts to your phone.
  • Technical & security metadata — login session records (IP address, device/user-agent, timestamps) and rate-limit counters used to keep the service secure.
  • Preferences — language preference and your map-display consent choice.

We do not collect your phone's own GPS location, your contacts, photos, files, advertising identifiers, or payment data.

3. Why we process it (purposes & legal basis)

  • Provide the tracking service: live machine positions, trip history, telemetry, alerts and reports — performance of the contract with your organisation.
  • Deliver push alerts (geofence, theft, offline, expiry warnings).
  • Security, abuse prevention and auditing — legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and accountable.
  • Customer support and service communications.
  • Compliance with legal obligations, including retention required for accounting or lawful requests.

4. Map & geocoding services — explicit consent

When you open a machine's map in the app, the app first asks for your consent. Only if you allow it:

  • Map tiles are loaded from the OpenStreetMap tile service (openstreetmap.org), which sees your device's IP address and the map area you view.
  • The machine's coordinates are sent to our backend, which forwards a rounded position (reduced precision, approx. 11 m) to our geocoding provider to convert coordinates into a place name. Your device never contacts the geocoding provider directly.

If you decline, no map or geocoding request is made and you can still view the raw coordinates inside the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by logging out (consent is stored per login) or on a fresh sign-in.

5. Who receives your data

  • Hosting infrastructure — our servers run on Vultr cloud infrastructure.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — receives your push token to deliver notifications.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation tile CDN — receives map tile requests only after your consent (section 4).
  • Geocoding provider — receives rounded machine coordinates via our backend, only after your consent.
  • Your organisation — administrators of your organisation can see account and machine data belonging to that organisation.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. Data may be transferred internationally where our providers operate; transfers rely on the providers' contractual safeguards.

6. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes. Access is controlled by role-based permissions and strict organisation (tenant) isolation — users can only see their own organisation's data. Administrative and security-relevant actions are recorded in audit logs.

7. Retention

  • Account & identity data — kept while your account is active; erased within 30 days of a processed deletion request or account termination.
  • Location & telemetry history — retained according to your organisation's plan and storage limits; older data is removed as retention windows roll forward.
  • Audit & command records — retained in pseudonymous form (user IDs without names/emails) for at least 12 months for security auditing and legal accountability.
  • Login sessions & push tokens — deleted immediately on logout, deactivation or erasure.

8. Your rights — including deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and to object to or withdraw consent for processing.

To request deletion of your account and personal data you can:

  • Submit a deletion request inside the app — Profile → Support & privacy → Request account deletion (you must be signed in), or
  • Email privacy@yantrago.com from the address associated with your account, or
  • Ask your organisation's YantraGO administrator.

Deletion is processed within 30 days. Erasure removes your name, email, phone number, credentials, sessions, push tokens and notification data, and deletes your customer record. We retain only pseudonymous audit and command records (section 7) where needed for security and legal obligations — they can no longer be linked to your identity. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account.

9. India — DPDP Act

For users in India, processing is carried out under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We process personal data for the specified, lawful purposes described above and, where required, on the basis of your consent (such as the map/geocoding consent in section 4). As a Data Principal you may exercise your rights of access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal by contacting privacy@yantrago.com. We aim to acknowledge grievances within 7 days and resolve them within 30 days.

10. Children

YantraGO is a business product and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the applicable minimum age.

11. Cookies & tracking

The admin console stores an authentication token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. We use no advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies.

12. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change and, for material changes, we will notify users in the app or by email. Questions, requests and complaints: privacy@yantrago.com or support@yantrago.com.
